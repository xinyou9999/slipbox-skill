# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds and skill levels. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Chinese and English contributions are welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information and spam are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and protect those affected.

Use a private maintainer contact exposed on the GitHub profile or a private reporting feature when available. If no private channel is available, request one without including personal details in public. Do not promise confidentiality through a public issue. This policy has no guaranteed response deadline.
